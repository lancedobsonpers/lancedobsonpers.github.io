Privacy Policy

Lance Dobson · Last updated October 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Lance Dobson ("we", "us") handles information in connection with the text-messaging (SMS) program operated from the phone number (385) 481-3188.

What the program is

The number sends personal notifications to its owner, Lance Dobson, from his own software: for example, confirmation that a note he texted in was saved, and reminders he has set. It is not a marketing program, and no other people are enrolled.

Information we collect

  • The mobile phone number that opts in to the program.
  • The content of text messages sent to and from the number, which is used only to provide the notifications described above.
  • Opt-in and opt-out records (when START or STOP was sent), kept to honor your choices.

How information is used

Information is used only to send the messages you asked for, to respond to HELP requests, and to honor STOP requests. It is processed by our messaging provider (Twilio) solely to deliver messages.

Sharing

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Mobile phone numbers and SMS consent are not shared with anyone except the messaging provider needed to deliver the messages.

Your choices

Reply STOP to (385) 481-3188 at any time to stop receiving messages. Reply HELP for help. Message and data rates may apply.

Security and retention

Information is stored on systems controlled by Lance Dobson and kept only as long as needed to provide the program or meet legal obligations.
